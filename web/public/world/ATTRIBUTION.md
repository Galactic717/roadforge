# World data

- `earth-night.jpg`: NASA Earth Observatory, Black Marble 2016, 3600 × 1800. Downloaded unchanged from https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144898/BlackMarble_2016_01deg.jpg . Source: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps/ . NASA imagery is generally public domain; no NASA endorsement is implied.
- `tokyo-tiles.json`: endpoint metadata from the official PLATEAU data catalog, https://api.plateauview.mlit.go.jp/datacatalog/plateau-datasets . Tokyo 2025 Minato, Chuo, and Koto textured buildings. The catalog also retains bridge and road records, but those surface meshes are not displayed because their broad untextured surfaces hide aerial road detail. Streamed data is owned by the relevant local authority, provided under CC BY 4.0 / PLATEAU policy: https://www.mlit.go.jp/plateau/site-policy/ . These are surveyed, textured city models, not Google photogrammetry.
- Tokyo terrain: PLATEAU / Mapterhorn / Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI), https://docs.plateauview.mlit.go.jp/datasets/terrain/ . Official public endpoint requires no token. Attribution remains on screen.
- Tokyo orthophotos: GSI nationwide seamless aerial imagery, https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/ichiran.html . Streamed from the official GSI tile service at supported zoom levels through 18. PLATEAU-Ortho 2023 does not cover Tokyo.
- Global fallback satellite imagery: Esri World Imagery. Provider-supplied attribution remains on screen, including capture.
- Optional Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles and Cesium World Terrain use the user's restricted browser credential. Provider-supplied attributions remain on screen, including capture. No map provider credentials are included in these files.
